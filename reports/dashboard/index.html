How to read this

  • Data: Online is MTGO. It publishes only Challenge top-8 brackets and a sample of 5-0 League lists, so it has many decklists but few matches, all between strong decks. Paper is Melee, CardsRealm and Topdeck, which publish every round. Most paper events are in Italy and Brazil.
  • Win rate counts matches, not games, excludes mirrors, and counts draws as half a win.
  • Intervals are 95% Wilson intervals. Two decks whose intervals overlap a lot can't be told apart yet.
  • Online vs paper is flagged when the two intervals don't overlap, so the scenes clearly disagree.
  • Archetypes come from the community rules in MTGOFormatData. Source data: fbettega/MTG_decklistcache.
